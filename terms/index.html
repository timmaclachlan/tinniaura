⚠️ Important Medical Disclaimer

TinniAura is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. TinniAura provides acoustic sound masking, relaxation soundscapes, and pitch-matching tools designed to assist in sound habituation.

If you experience sudden hearing loss, pulsating tinnitus, dizziness, ear pain, or sudden change in your hearing, seek immediate evaluation from an Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) specialist or certified audiologist.

1. Safe Listening Guidelines

To protect your hearing, TinniAura incorporates an internal 85 dB safety limiter. However, prolonged exposure to high-volume sound through headphones can cause permanent hearing damage. We strongly recommend:

  • Listening at the lowest volume level that provides comfortable habituation relief.
  • Never turning up masking sounds to fully drown out loud ambient environments.
  • Taking regular listening breaks during prolonged sessions.
