Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Strict Zero Data Collection Policy

1. Privacy-First Acoustic Architecture

TinniAura was architected on a fundamental privacy principle: your medical and auditory health data belongs exclusively to you. We do not operate remote tracking servers, do not collect personal analytics, and do not profile your listening sessions.

2. Apple HealthKit Integration

If you grant optional permission to Apple HealthKit, TinniAura reads your clinical audiogram data (hearing thresholds in dB HL) strictly to compute personal Equal Loudness calibration curves on your device.

  • Your audiogram data never leaves your device.
  • No hearing data is ever transmitted to us or any third party.
  • You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > TinniAura.

3. Audio Microphone & Sensor Data

TinniAura generates synthetic audio signals on-device using 32-bit CoreAudio synthesis. TinniAura never accesses, records, or transmits through your device microphone.

4. In-App Purchases & Apple ID

All in-app purchases are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit. We never receive, store, or have access to your credit card, billing address, or Apple ID account details.
