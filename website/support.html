Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my lifetime purchase on a new iPhone or iPad?

Open TinniAura, tap Settings (or tap the PRO button in the header), and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase will automatically sync with Apple's App Store servers with zero charge.

Are there any recurring subscriptions or hidden monthly fees?

No. TinniAura is strictly Pay Once, Keep Forever. One purchase unlocks all Pro soundscapes, notched filtering, independent dual-ear matching, and future sound updates permanently.

Does acoustic masking play while my screen is locked or in the background?

Yes! TinniAura integrates with iOS background audio and the Lock Screen Now Playing center. You can close the app or lock your iPhone, and your sleep timer will continue to fade out smoothly.

How do I safely match my ringing pitch with the shuttle wheel?

Put on wired or Bluetooth headphones in a quiet room. Select the Sine tone, gently rotate the Spring Shuttle Wheel until the pitch matches the center pitch of your ear ringing, and adjust volume so the tone sits at or slightly below your tinnitus level.

How does Apple HealthKit integration work?

TinniAura can import hearing threshold levels from clinical audiograms stored in Apple Health to apply Equal Loudness calibration, keeping high frequencies smooth and soothing.
