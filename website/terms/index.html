2. Safe Listening Practices & Decibel Limits

Acoustic sound therapy should always be conducted at safe, comfortable listening levels:

  • Never set masking audio louder than your perceived tinnitus sound. Sound therapy is designed to mix gently with your ringing, not drown it out at harmful decibels.
  • TinniAura incorporates an algorithmic 85 dB Hearing Safety Limiter. However, you remain responsible for your device volume output and protecting your auditory health.
  • If you experience ear pain, fullness, dizziness, or hyperacusis spikes, stop playback immediately and consult an ear specialist.
