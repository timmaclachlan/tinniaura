Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

🔒 Complete On-Device Privacy Guarantee

TinniAura is engineered from the ground up to operate completely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell your personal, biometric, audiogram, or sound therapy listening history to third parties.

1. Apple HealthKit & Audiology Data (Strict Apple Guideline 5.1 Compliance)

TinniAura offers optional integration with Apple HealthKit to calibrate sound therapy gain curves against your clinical audiograms and record completed therapy sessions as Mindful Minutes.

  • Audiogram Data: When permission is granted, TinniAura reads your audiogram hearing threshold points locally to calculate loudness compensation. This data never leaves your device.
  • Mindful Sessions: Completed sound masking sessions can be written to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes at your discretion.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, HealthKit data is never shared with third-party tracking services, advertising platforms, or data brokers.

2. Anonymous Usage & Telemetry

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs. TinniAura uses a minimal, privacy-first local telemetry mechanism solely to detect audio crashes or decibel safety limit triggers on device.

3. In-App Purchases & Apple ID

Transactions for TinniAura Pro (Once-Off Lifetime Unlock) are handled exclusively by Apple's StoreKit 2 framework. TinniAura never collects, accesses, or stores your credit card details, billing address, or payment credentials.

4. Diagnostic Doctor PDF Reports

When you export a clinical summary report for your ENT or audiologist containing your Tinnitus Handicap Inventory (THI) or Tinnitus Functional Index (TFI) scores, the PDF is generated 100% locally on your iOS device via CoreGraphics. You choose where to share, airDrop, or print the document.
